# Foodics – Run Your Restaurant Smarter

An independent academic concept for a B2B LinkedIn marketing campaign for Foodics, developed as a Marketing student portfolio project. The campaign introduces restaurant owners and managers to practical ways restaurant technology can support daily operations.

## Campaign objective
Build awareness of Foodics among restaurant decision-makers in Saudi Arabia and encourage interested businesses to learn more.

## Target audience
Restaurant and café owners, small and medium-sized food businesses, and restaurant managers in Saudi Arabia.

## Campaign concept
“Run Your Restaurant Smarter” connects familiar daily tasks—orders, kitchen coordination, inventory and reporting—with Foodics’ restaurant management capabilities. The campaign uses a helpful, clear tone and avoids unsupported performance promises.
